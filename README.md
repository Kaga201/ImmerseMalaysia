# Immerse Malaysia – XR Tourism App Prototype (Alpha)

One tourism app for a destination (demo: **Melaka**) with 5 connected XR functions:

1. **Explore** – AR heritage trail, Time-Travel Lens (A Famosa 1512 vs today), audio guide (English)
2. **Learn** – the history of Melaka in 3D/AR (Stadthuys, Flor de la Mar, Sultanate Palace), VR virtual visit of Dutch Square, quizzes, group/school-trip mode (demo code: MLK-4821)
3. **Clean & Health** – cleanliness ratings for toilets and food stalls, air quality, crowd level, AR guide to the nearest clean toilet or clinic, report a dirty spot
4. **Shop Local** – best local products of Melaka in 3D/AR (Nyonya beaded slippers, tiffin carrier, palm sugar) and a seller view
5. **Safety** – flood and disaster alerts for the destination, AR water-height view, AR route to the relief centre, VR flood drill

Open the hosted link on a phone to use camera AR ("View in AR"). The VR visit can enter VR in a headset browser.
Sample data is used for ratings, river levels and group results in this alpha version.
